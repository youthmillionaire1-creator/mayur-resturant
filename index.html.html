Our Story

Cooked with Passion,
Served with Love

Founded in 2010 in the heart of Ahmedabad, Mayur Restaurant has been serving authentic Indian flavours to over 50,000 happy guests. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into one of CG Road's most beloved dining destinations.

Our chefs bring generations of culinary knowledge to every dish — from slow-simmered curries to perfectly charred tandoor breads. We source only the freshest local produce and finest spices, every single day.
